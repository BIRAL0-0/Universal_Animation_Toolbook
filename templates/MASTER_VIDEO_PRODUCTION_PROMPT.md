# MASTER VIDEO PRODUCTION PROMPT — reusable template

**Status: PERMANENT SYSTEM FILE.** This is the template every new video starts from. A video is
produced by copying this into `projects/<NNN>-<slug>/PROMPT.md`, customising every `{{...}}` slot
with that video's own research, and saving the finalised prompt **before** production begins.

Do not edit this file for one video's benefit. If a production exposes a missing instruction,
improve this template and record the change in
[`docs/production/PIPELINE_CHANGELOG.md`](../docs/production/PIPELINE_CHANGELOG.md).

Governing rules: [`AGENT_OPERATING_RULES.md`](../AGENT_OPERATING_RULES.md).
Machinery: [`universal_toolbook/`](../universal_toolbook/).
Worked example: [`projects/video-001-twenty-three-minutes/PROMPT.md`](../projects/video-001-twenty-three-minutes/PROMPT.md).

---

## 0. Mission

Produce one complete, watchable, production-ready MP4 {{FORMAT_LINE, e.g. "stickman animation as a
YouTube Short (1080×1920, 9:16, ≤180 s)"}} from research through render and QA, inside this
repository, using the reusable pipeline. The deliverable is a **video file** — not a plan, not a
script, not a still. Do not stop at recommendations, reports, scripts, storyboards or sample
images.

## 2. Research constraints on the script

- {{FACT_1}} — confidence {{HIGH|MEDIUM-HIGH|MEDIUM}} → {{MAY/MAY NOT}} be asserted in narration.
- {{FACT_2}} — confidence {{...}} → {{...}}

## 3. Creative direction

Adopt these defaults unless this video's research overrides them; record any override.

1. Runtime {{TARGET_RUNTIME}}; beats of 10–20 s.
2. Hook lands inside the first 10 seconds; start in the middle of the premise.
3. **One metaphor, carried to exhaustion, never explained.** State it here: {{METAPHOR}}.
4. Narration register: {{e.g. "documentary narrator + N short character lines"}}. No lecturing.
5. Where the argument allows, prove it by showing the same conditions with one variable changed.
6. **Mute-legibility test:** the film must be understandable with the audio off.
7. Close on a direct-address question, originally worded.
8. Music is load-bearing and shifts mood per act, ducked under narration.
9. Chapter labels double as on-screen cards and as YouTube chapters in the delivery notes.
10. **Originality:** no character, script, scene, chapter list or metaphor reproduced from any
    reference. References inform craft only.

## 5. Animation style

- Style {{cel|parchment}}; zero film grain.
- Movement must come from the engine's supported action list
  (`universal_toolbook/engine/motion_library.js`). **No scene may rely on a static pose plus a
  zoom.**
- Camera: speech-locked `camera_shots` with `cue_on` so pushes and pans land on the spoken beat.
- Information is carried by supported boards/props
  (`universal_toolbook/engine/world_props_and_boards.js`), not invented ones. If a needed visual
  has no primitive, either substitute an existing one and log the substitution, or add the
  primitive to the engine reusably — never fake it per-video.

## 7. Narration voice and settings

| Speaker | Engine | Voice | Rate | Pitch | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| narrator | Kokoro | `am_michael` | +1% | −1Hz | repository PRIMARY default |
| {{char}} | Kokoro | {{voice}} | {{rate}} | {{pitch}} | {{why}} |

- Engine priority: **Kokoro first**, Piper only as fallback.
- Natural speed, correct pronunciation, audible pauses, expressive delivery, consistent volume,
  no rushing, no clipping.
- Polish with [`docs/production-workflow/prompts/VOICEOVER_POLISH_PROMPT.md`](../docs/production-workflow/prompts/VOICEOVER_POLISH_PROMPT.md)
  **exactly as it exists, byte-for-byte**. Save `SCRIPT_ORIGINAL.md`, `SCRIPT_POLISHED.md`,
  `SCRIPT_CHANGELOG.md`. The change log must give, per change, the line touched and the rule that
  justified it.
- After approval the polished text is copied verbatim into `episode.json`. No editing between
  approval and render.

## 8. Audio requirements

- BGM mode `dynamic`, crossfading per-scene tracks from the bundled Creative Commons catalogue
  (`universal_toolbook/assets/bgm/BGM_CATALOG.json`). Act mapping: {{ACT_MAP}}.
- Speech ducking on; narration clearly above music and effects.
- Foley derived from scene events; add environmental ambience and transition/emphasis sounds.
- **Mix targets, measured on the rendered WAV:** peak −3…−1 dBFS, RMS −16…−19 dBFS. The engine
  normalises loudness automatically (fast peak limiter + bounded make-up, KI-008/PC-010); if a
  master still measures outside the band, fix the engine and re-render — never tweak one video's
  gains by hand, and never ship on peak alone (RMS is recorded in every report since PC-010).
- Record the licence and attribution of every track used in `ASSET_MANIFEST.json`.

## 9. Export settings

| | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | {{YouTube Shorts 9:16 \| Regular YouTube 16:9}} | {{the other}} |
| Resolution | {{1080×1920 \| 1920×1080}} | {{the other}} |
| Frame rate | 30 fps | 30 fps |
| Codec | H.264 High + AAC 44.1 kHz stereo | same |
| Preset / CRF | `veryfast` / `crf 24` | same |
| CLI flag | {{--shorts \| --aspect-ratio 9:16 \| --aspect-ratio 16:9}} | the other |
| Workers / chunk | 2 / 45 s, `--resume` on | same |
| Output | `projects/<slug>/final.mp4` | `projects/<slug>/renders/<name>.mp4` |

Important visuals inside mobile-safe areas; inspect portrait stills before the master render.
**Do not hand-author `portrait_focus` / `portrait_focus_x` / board `portrait_w` / `portrait_x` as a
first instinct (KI-009):** the engine already auto-focuses the active character and re-fits every
board into the safe crop (`fitBoardToPortraitViewport`). Overrides are a last resort, added only
after a draft frame at a specific timestamp proves a framing defect, and logged with that evidence.
Note that stills render without TTS timings, so cue-locked boards can only be judged from draft or
master frames at `board_pop + ~1.6 s` (KI-003).

## 10. Quality requirements — all must actually pass, with evidence

1. `run_studio.py validate` reports **`warnings: []`** before any render.
2. Portrait (and landscape, if delivered) stills inspected for every scene: key visuals readable,
   boards inside the safe crop, no name-tag collision, no clipped limbs.
3. Draft render reviewed for timing, board build, camera cuts, subtitle speaker and highlight.
4. Master decodes end-to-end: `ffmpeg -v error -i final.mp4 -f null -` exits clean.
5. Measured container matches spec: dimensions, fps, duration inside target, one video + one
   audio stream.
6. **No black-screen sections** — sampled frames across the whole timeline are non-uniform.
7. Audio measured on the mixed WAV: peak and RMS inside target, no unintended interior silence.
8. Frames extracted and **actually looked at** at ≥6 timestamps; findings written into
   `QA_REPORT.md`.
9. Mute-legibility test: the extracted frame sequence tells the story without audio.
10. `tests/production/` and `tests/run_all_tests.js` pass.

**Never claim a visual or audio check passed if it was not performed.**

## 12. Self-healing obligation

Any failure is recorded in `PRODUCTION_LOG.md`, root-caused, fixed **reusably**, covered by a
regression check in `tests/production/`, and written up in `docs/production/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`,
`docs/production/LESSONS_LEARNED.md` and `docs/production/PIPELINE_CHANGELOG.md`. A failure is
never reported resolved without verification. Recurring problems are never hidden.

## 13. Delivery report

At the end provide: final MP4 · title and selected topic · original and polished scripts ·
thumbnail or preview where supported · project folder and downloadable archive · production and
QA report · remaining limitations.
