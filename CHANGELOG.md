### 2026-11-11 — v4.4.0 Phone-Size Thumbnail Proof, Seven Title Strategies, SEO Package & Post-Render Creative Review
- **Covers are now rendered at the size they are actually seen (`tools/thumbnail_legibility.py`)**: every thumbnail is rendered at 168 px (mobile search) and 216 px (mobile feed) onto a labelled contact sheet, so illegibility can be seen instead of guessed. The tool gates on the one measure that proved reliable — two covers being the same picture at phone size (calibrated: identical/rescaled/brightened ≈ 0.00, mirrored 0.010, unrelated ≈ 1.0; threshold 0.15) — and deliberately does **not** gate on legibility itself. Three candidate scalars were tested against the eight shipped covers and each confidently failed covers that read perfectly at 168 px; the reasoning and measurements are recorded in `docs/production/CREATIVE_PACKAGING.md` §2.1 rather than hidden.
- **Title generation across seven strategies (`tools/creative_gate.py`)**: candidates now carry an `approach` label — `curiosity_gap`, `question`, `outcome_number`, `contradiction`, `stakes_loss`, `identity`, `reversal` — and at least four distinct strategies must be represented, so three rewordings of one idea no longer count as a comparison. All eight shipped packages were relabelled from what each candidate actually is; candidates added during the audit are marked `drafted_in_review: true` so no record claims they were considered before the film was made.
- **The full SEO package is a required field set (`record.json`)**: `primary_keyword`, `secondary_keywords` (1–3), `title_alternates` (≥ 2, never the title in use), `hashtags` (3–5, with the `#`) and `category`. All eight packages backfilled from their own published descriptions and tags, not invented.
- **Separate post-render creative review (`tools/creative_review.py`)**: reviews the shipped MP4 against its own record — whether the opening moves or is narration over a still, where the film goes visually static, whether the promised reveal and payoff exist at the times the retention map claims, and whether the record describes the file it shipped with. It saves five frames per film for human inspection, and it fails only on contradictions with the record. Its cover-luminance comparison is reported, never enforced, because it fired on four covers that `thumbnail_style.py` rates MATCH — a designed cover is supposed to be brighter than an average film frame.
- **CI, docs and registry**: both new gates wired into `.github/workflows/verify.yml` (5 jobs, 43 steps); tools listed in `master_prompt.md` §3.3 and the stage-22 row of the production architecture; procedure added to `skills/youtube-packaging/SKILL.md`; schema updated in `docs/production/CREATIVE_PACKAGING.md`; new tools registered in `tools/validate_structure.py` (73 required files). Tests: 27 creative-gate contracts (10 new), 8 legibility contracts, 8 review contracts — **136 passed** across `tests/unit` and `tests/production`.

### 2026-11-11 — v4.3.0 Master Operating Manual, Creative Packaging System & Documentation Truth Pass
- **Root operating manual (`master_prompt.md`)**: New 1,100-line authoritative manual that eliminates rediscovery — ten operating principles each named with the failure it prevents, a full architecture map with permanent/temporary lifetimes, the 23-stage production architecture with a Mermaid flowchart, a CLI reference **extracted from actual `--help` output and source** (including three tools documented as `--help`-broken), the standard episode-route workflow with runnable commands, format policy, visual identity, voice policy, publishing package and record schema, skills system, QA gates, four-level documentation hierarchy with a full index, session-start protocol, task routing, knowledge-extraction loop, CI map and acceptance criteria.
- **Creative packaging system (`tools/creative_gate.py`)**: The repository could prove a film *rendered* but not that anyone would click. Every `videos/<slug>/record.json` now carries a `creative` block — ≥3 hook candidates with a stated reason for the choice, a retention map with hook/escalation/reveal/payoff purposes, ≥3 title approaches, ≥3 thumbnail concepts with different focal subjects, title/thumbnail/hook alignment, `M`/`O`/`P` labelled claims, and a ten-criterion script critique with a named weakest point. The gate fails a video that cannot show its rejected alternatives, rejects generic openers, checks that the hook's words are actually spoken in the film, and refuses a prediction presented as a fact. It states plainly that it cannot judge whether a hook is *good*.
- **All eight shipped videos backfilled and indicted where honest (`videos/*/record.json`)**: Blocks were written from each film's real `script.md` and shipped thumbnail. Two films score 2/5 on their own opening hook and are recorded that way rather than scored away — `the-first-plank` opens on a desk instead of a problem, `the-two-inch-bridge` opens on scale instead of stakes. Both are flagged for a future re-edit without re-rendering. The exercise also found a factual conflict: `discipline-vs-obsession`'s title promises "9 PM vs 2 AM" while the rendered clock reads 9 PM and 1 AM.
- **New skill and evidence doc (`skills/youtube-packaging/SKILL.md`, `docs/production/CREATIVE_PACKAGING.md`)**: The 17th skill closes a real gap — no skill previously covered thumbnails at all, beyond a single mention in `youtube-research`. The doc owns the `creative` schema and an explicit three-way split of what is automated, what needs editorial judgement and what is impossible without analytics access.
- **Documentation truth pass**: `README.md` no longer claims a `projects/` directory exists, no longer says "six skills" (there are 17), and its quick start now shows the episode route that actually produced every shipped film, with the project route demoted to an optional scaffold. `AGENTS.md` §10 no longer hard-codes one session's branch name. `START_HERE.md` now points at the manual first. `templates/MASTER_VIDEO_PRODUCTION_PROMPT.md` gained a §3a creative-strategy section that must be filled before writing, and its publish gate now runs all five gates in order.
- **Gates and tests**: `tools/creative_gate.py` wired into `.github/workflows/verify.yml` (5 jobs, 41 steps); registry and activation map updated in `tools/validate_structure.py` and `AGENTS.md` (17 skills, 71 required files); new `tests/production/test_creative_gate.py` with 17 tests covering the honest pass plus every dishonest shortcut the gate exists to catch. Full suite: 110 passed (`tests/unit` + `tests/production`), docs check 2478 references across 109 files.

### 2026-11-10 — v4.2.0 YouTube Publishing Packages, Designed Thumbnails & Permanent Script Archive
- **Publishing packages replace anonymous MP4s (`videos/`)**: Every completed video now lives at `videos/<slug>/<slug>.mp4` with `script.md`, `thumbnail.jpg` and `record.json`, plus a compact `videos/index.json` entry. The six existing videos were migrated from `videos/video-001..006/final.mp4` to `what-if-every-notification-cost-you-23-minutes`, `discipline-vs-obsession`, `the-first-plank`, `the-two-inch-bridge`, `the-two-second-reach` and `never-check-the-time-at-3-am`.
- **Final scripts retained permanently (`videos/<slug>/script.md`)**: Correction to the 2026-11-09 lean policy — the approved script is the canonical creative record and is never deleted. Each file carries the final title, format/runtime header, logline, scene divisions with timing where the master has chapters, the exact narration and dialogue from the rendered mix, and production notes.
- **Designed thumbnails, not frame grabs (`videos/<slug>/thumbnail.jpg`)**: Concept developed per video from the central visual metaphor, generated as cel-stickman base art, text overlaid in Poppins-Bold where it improved the concept, then inspected at full size *and* at mobile preview size. Six approved assets: 1280×720 for the 16:9 long-form masters, 1080×1920 for the 9:16 Shorts, all well under YouTube's 2 MB limit.
- **Full YouTube metadata per video (`record.json`)**: Added `slug`, `filename`, `script`, `thumbnail`, `youtube_description` (opening, premise, reason to watch, true search terms, hashtags, CTA, verified CC music attribution), `pinned_comment` (a genuine discussion question tied to the film), `aspect_ratio`, `resolution`, `duration_seconds`, `summary`, `key_angle`, `status`, `directory`, `thumbnail_spec` and `content_fingerprint`. Legacy keys (`duration_sec`, `final_video`, `script_summary`, `tags`) are preserved so existing tooling keeps working, and the schema supports both portrait and landscape masters.
- **New publishing gate (`tools/validate_publishing.py`)**: Verifies the MP4 exists and probes (duration, width, height) against the recorded format, that `script.md` carries the title and narration, that `thumbnail.jpg` is platform-sized and under 2 MB, that `record.json` holds every required field with non-empty values, that the index entry and content fingerprint match the bytes on disk, and that no temporary/unfinished file sits in the package. Wired into `.github/workflows/verify.yml` and covered by `tests/unit/test_publishing_package.py`.
- **Duplicate detection across the new index (`universal_toolbook/config/duplicate_detection.py`)**: `check_duplicate` now also compares `key_angle`, reads the renamed `summary` field (legacy `script_summary` still honoured), and accepts `allow_remake=` so an explicitly requested sequel or remake can clear the gate.
- **Structure rules tightened (`tools/validate_structure.py`, `tests/unit/test_repository_structure.py`, `tools/check_docs.py`)**: Completed video directories must be descriptive slugs and must ship `<slug>.mp4` + `script.md` + `thumbnail.jpg` + `record.json`; generic names such as `final.mp4` are now a hard failure. Historical docs and changelog entries still resolve through legacy aliases.
- **Policy and docs (`universal_toolbook/process/09_LEAN_REPOSITORY_POLICY.md`, `docs/production/YOUTUBE_PUBLISHING.md`, `AGENTS.md`)**: Lean policy rewritten around the five permanent artifacts (MP4, script, thumbnail, record, index entry); Stage 13 promoted from PARTIAL to package-complete/manual-upload with the validator as its gate.
