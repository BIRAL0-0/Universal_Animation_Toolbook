### 2026-11-10 — v4.11.0 Secondary Motion for Held Poses, with Deliberate Stillness Controls
- **KI-020 — stationary actions were nearly freeze-frames even after `idle_stand` improved.** An isolated render audit of 15 actions measured character-region median frame delta: `sit_bench` 0.132, `hold_blueprint` 0.213, `cross_arms_skeptical` 0.290, `listen_nod` 0.431 and `look_horizon` 0.491, against `sit_desk_type` 1.969. A shared, deterministic layer now adds subtle weight shift, head attention, arm resettling and gaze movement to 17 held actions; locomotion and already-moving gestures are excluded. It is applied once on both steady and blended evaluator paths. `idle_stand` no longer double-drives itself with a private oscillator set.
- **Beat boundaries ease instead of snapping.** Motion is actor-seeded (not beat-seeded), and an eased 0.4 s envelope fades the layer at beat edges. The outgoing stationary layer blends away on handoff to a moving action. Same-frame repeat checks are deterministic; transition probes showed no additional secondary-motion pop.
- **Stillness is authored, not hard-coded.** `secondary_motion: "full" | "subtle" | "none"` is accepted on an actor or an individual beat. `full` is the backwards-compatible default; `subtle` scales the layer to 0.35; `none` suppresses only the shared layer and retains the action's base-pose motion. Beat values override actor-level values. This supports intentional suspense, shock or dramatic pauses without disabling the whole animation system.
- **Evidence and limits.** Re-rendered 15-action audit after the shared layer: held-pose results rose to 0.53–1.04; already-moving controls stayed at their pre-layer values. Six frame strips (`idle_stand`, `sit_bench`, `hold_blueprint`, `cross_arms_skeptical`, `listen_nod`, `look_horizon`) were visually reviewed. Frame-difference magnitude is a motion diagnostic, not a naturalness or quality score: the layer makes a hold visibly alive, not expressive; pose choice and story fit remain editorial judgment, and `none` does not freeze base action motion.
- Tests: behavioral Node-backed coverage for deterministic evaluation, default/actor/beat precedence, all three modes, moving-action exclusion and transition continuity; static authoring contract updated to prevent a second private idle layer.

### 2026-11-13 — v4.10.0 The Rules That Were Making the Films Look Static
- **The question was "are there stricter rules that keep making the video worse?" There were three, stacked, and a gate blind to all of them.** `the-first-plank` v3 satisfied every check in the repo and still read as a still image.
- **KI-019 — a non-speaking character was frozen solid.** `motion_library` set `speakBoost = 0.0` for anyone not mid-line. Of the 378 dialogue lines across `examples/`, only **112 (30%)** are spoken by a character, so for 70% of every film every on-screen actor had no body language at all. `LISTEN_BOOST = 0.45` now treats a listener as an actor; `force_gesture` still opts a beat into 0.65.
- **`idle_stand` — the pose nearly every hold resolves to — animated only a ribcage breath.** Measured on an 8 s probe, character-region frame delta was **0.500** against **9.753** for `walk`: a 20× gap. It now carries weight shift, head attention and arm resettle on four desynchronised periods. Re-rendered at 0.601 median (p90 0.881 → 1.418), and the frame strip shows a figure shifting onto one hip and turning its head instead of standing with its arms at its sides.
- **The camera rules were the trap, not the cure.** `video_renderer` demanded a **0.10** zoom delta for a shot to be significant, so **169 of 192 (88%)** authored shots had their zoom smeared across an entire scene; `episode.js` additionally gave every camera-less scene a silent `zoom_to ?? 1.04` creep. That creep moved enough pixels to clear `creative_review`'s absolute `0.0015` threshold — which is why the gate reported **0% static on a slideshow**. Thresholds are now `0.015/0.015/0.02` with a 2.6 s settle, the scene-level camera path shares the significance test it never had, and the default creep is gone. Verified by rendering an A/B: the camera fix alone changed no pixels, which is what pointed the investigation at the rig.
- **A schema validator cannot see that a pose never changes.** `validate_specs` gains a `MAX_SINGLE_BEAT_SEC = 6.0` motion budget. It found **15 specs** holding one pose for a whole scene — `discipline-vs-obsession` held `celebrate_arms_up` for **64.8 s**. Long single beats are split into two changing actions, and two beats that ran past their scene end were clamped.
- **The review gate measured the wrong pixels.** `creative_review` now measures relative motion in the **stage band only** (35%–78% of height, 0.40× median, 2.5 s minimum run) and reports `median_frame_delta`, `dead_runs_sec` and `dead_share`. Whole-frame differencing could not see a static stage because the typing board and subtitle glow change every frame.
- **Honest limits, recorded in KI-019.** Locomotion still moves ~10× more than any rest pose; closing that is per-action animation work, not configuration. Which action belongs at a story beat is human judgment. The long `discipline-vs-obsession` scenes were re-timed mechanically (55/45 split into a neighbouring action) — plausible, not authored.
- Tests: **250 passed**. `tests/unit/test_static_authoring.py` pins the listen boost, the idle oscillator count, the motion budget and that re-timed beats stay a valid partition of their scene.
