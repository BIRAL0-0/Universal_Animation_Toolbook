**The decisive argument [P]:** the crowded videos answer *"what happens if you stop?"*. This one
answers *"what is happening right now, every time you don't stop?"* It reframes a familiar subject
around a mechanism the audience has never had named for them, and it hands them something they can
act on in the next ten seconds. That is a stronger curiosity gap than another countdown.

## Why the title is worded this way

- **"What If"** — the measured winning grammar in this niche (refs 2, and every competitor above).
- **"Every Notification"** — the unit is small and universal; the viewer has one in their pocket.
- **"23 Minutes"** — a specific, odd, verifiable number beats a vague one. It creates the gap
  between *what I think it costs (seconds)* and *what it actually costs (23 minutes)*.
- Spoken title in narration uses the rounded **"twenty three minutes"**; the precise measured
  figure, 23 min 15 s, is recorded in `RESEARCH.md` §5 and cited in the end-card source note.

## Format decision

Per the master brief's default rule (Shorts 9:16 for short-form) **and** the measured evidence
that the reference channel's Short out-distributed its long-form film by ~166× **[O]**, the
delivery is a **vertical 9:16 Short at ~2:40**. That runtime deliberately mirrors the 2:40 of the
breakout comparable. A 16:9 landscape master is also rendered from the same spec, since the engine
reframes rather than stretches, so one authoring pass yields both surfaces.

## Known risks going in

1. **Two-character ceiling.** The engine's practical maximum is two characters per scene. The
   script is written for one protagonist plus one colleague who appears in two scenes only.
2. **No floating HUD.** There is no "counter above the head" primitive. The counter is realised as
   an in-world `digital_clock` prop plus `stat_cards` boards — a deliberate substitution, recorded
   in `PRODUCTION_LOG.md`.
3. **Portrait safe areas.** Boards must sit inside the 9:16 safe crop. Portrait stills are
   inspected before any master render.
4. **Factual overreach.** Mitigated by the script policy in `RESEARCH.md` §5: only High and
   Medium-High confidence claims are asserted; the daily focus budget is explicitly hypothetical.
