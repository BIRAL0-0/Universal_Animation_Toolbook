# The Bamboo Seed: 5 Years of Nothing, Then Everything (A Parable)

*Ship title used for publishing.*

**Moral:** "Nothing was wasted. It was growing where no one could see. Your hard work is never invisible. Keep going."

**Format:** portrait 9:16, Shorts-style. **Style:** warm parchment ink.

---

## S01 — The Seed

**NARRATOR:** A farmer planted a bamboo seed.

He dug a hole the size of his fist, set the seed in it, and pressed the soil down with his thumb.

**NARRATOR:** Then he watered it.

The sun came up. The sun went down. Nothing happened.

**NARRATOR:** Nobody clapped. Nobody noticed. There was nothing to notice.

---

## S02 — Year One

**NARRATOR:** A year went by.

Every morning he watered that patch of bare ground. Every evening he watered it again.

**NARRATOR:** Above the ground, the measurement did not change. Zero.

A neighbour leaned over the fence and watched him do it.

**NEIGHBOUR:** You are watering dirt.

---

## S03 — The Same Ground

**NARRATOR:** Two years. Three years. Four.

He kept the same appointment with the same square of earth.

**NARRATOR:** The neighbour stopped watching. There is nothing interesting about a man who waters dirt.

And underneath — quietly, patiently, in a place no one had thought to look —

**NARRATOR:** the seed was doing all of its work.

---

## S04 — The Roots

**NARRATOR:** Roots. Wider every season. Deeper every rain.

Nothing above the ground would have told you.

**NARRATOR:** The ground line stayed flat, and the measurement stayed at zero, and the whole time the work was real.

It was happening in the one place nobody was looking.

---

## S05 — Year Five

**NARRATOR:** Then, in the fifth year, the sun came up on a shape that had not been there the night before.

**NARRATOR:** It came up three feet before breakfast.

It came up so fast that the neighbour heard it and came back out to the fence.

**NEIGHBOUR:** What did you do?

**NARRATOR:** Nothing. He had done nothing different. That was the thing.

---

## S06 — The Moral

**NARRATOR:** The bamboo did not grow in six weeks. It grew for five years and showed up in six weeks.

The farmer stood under it and looked up at all that green, and he did not feel lucky.

**NARRATOR:** He felt like a man who had watered a seed every morning for five years.

**NARRATOR:** Nothing was wasted. It was growing where no one could see.

Your hard work is never invisible. Keep going.
