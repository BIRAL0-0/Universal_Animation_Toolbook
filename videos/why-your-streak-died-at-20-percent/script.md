## Chapter One — Two Cards, Same Eight Washes

**S01 · Two cards**

## Chapter One — The Twenty-One-Day Lie

**S03 · The turn**

You did not quit because you were weak.
You quit because your card started at zero.
And someone has been telling you it takes twenty one days.
They are wrong, and it is not their fault.

**S04 · A dropped word**

In nineteen sixty, a plastic surgeon wrote a book.
He noticed his patients took about twenty one days to get used to a new face.
He wrote a minimum of about twenty one days.
Somewhere along the way, the word minimum fell off the truck.
A surgeon's note about faces became a rule about habits.

## Chapter Two — What They Actually Measured

**S05 · The range**

In twenty ten, ninety six people chose one small daily behaviour.
They repeated it in the same context for twelve weeks.
Time to feeling automatic ran from eighteen days to two hundred and fifty four.
The middle of that range was sixty six days.

**S06 · A different card**

For some of those volunteers, the card did not start at zero.
One group got a twelve stamp card with two bonus stamps.
The other got a plain ten stamp card.
Ten purchases either way.
The group that was already started finished first.

## Chapter Three — The Catch

**S07 · No free stamps for free**

So hand yourself two free stamps.
Here is the catch.
The effect disappeared when no reason was given for the head start.
Two squares filled in for no reason is just a lie you can see.
You need a reason the person will accept.

**S08 · The reason**

You already know how to open a conversation.
So do not start at the first sentence.
Open the document, write the second paragraph.
Two stamps before the work begins.

**S09 · Page twenty**

Start the book at page twenty.
Not the first page.
The card was never eight washes long.
The card started wherever someone told you it started.
